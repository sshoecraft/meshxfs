`tools/criteria.py:1465` (annotated_run), `tools/criteria.py:113-121` (board choice at import) and
`tools/criteria.py:189` (run_board, used by retire_cells at `tools/defects.py:1187`).

**What the cited evidence measures.** Every control lap log shows the same refusals. In
`tests/evidence/batch_laps/b20261023-e1901e/20261023T155737Z-4addc500.log`:

## Verdict

**REJECT.**

For remove --fixed, the cause must be proven by instrumented evidence. The cited control proves
only the parse-level part of the cause: the first-two-colons split and parse_at's refusal of 1/any
and of the own-rig drbd key. With the switch off, every disposition is refused at the parse step,
so the control never reaches annotated_run, the import-time board choice or retire_cells. Three of
the four switched hunks therefore have no measured failure behind them. The annotated_run part rests
on code reading (`data/defects.json:2320`, "Read 2026-10-21"). The own-rig board-routing part rests
on a sentence in the record's own prose, a "records no run stamped 2026-10-22T00:35:43Z" refusal and
a removal that left the run listed, and no log of that measurement is cited or appears in any cited
file. The fix-on batch passing proves that the bundle works. It does not prove that each hunk
targets a proven cause.

What would cure it, as measured and preserved evidence cited by the proposal:

1. A control that leaves the parse hunk in and takes out the board-choice and run_board hunks
   (and, separately or together, the annotated_run hunk). Its lap logs must show, on
   `2/net/mesh/drbd:release:drbd:2026-10-22T00:35:43Z`, `criteria.py infra`/`amend` refused with
   "records no run stamped" and the removal leaving the run listed, and on the `1/any` and
   `2/net/mesh/direct` shapes the find() refusal that the annotated_run hunk addresses. Size the
   fix-on batch from that control. Alternatively, cite the preserved lap log of the 2026-10-23
   pre-change measurement if one exists.
2. Correct the cells statement: all 30 cells are already retired by the verify.
