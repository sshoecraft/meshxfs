### Recomputed

The formula is docs/release-process.md:123-124:
- `  L = BetaQuantile(δ; k, n − k + 1). When every calibration lap failed, L = δ^(1/n).`
- `- N = ⌈ln α / ln(1 − L)⌉. If the defect still failed at rate L, the chance that N laps would all`

With δ = α = 0.005 and k = 12, n = 20:
- Bisection of P(X ≥ 12 | 20, L) = 0.005 gives L = 0.299095. At that L the tail is 0.005000.
- ln 0.005 / ln(1 − L) = 14.91, so N = 15.
- (1 − L)^15 = 0.004840 ≤ α, while (1 − L)^14 = 0.006906 > α.
- The batch's `planned` (15) and `lower_bound` (0.299094724757627) match.
