## Scope and limits (part of this approval)

1. **Exits covered.** The batches measured exits where the lap returns and its EXIT trap runs to the
   end (inner lap rc=0 and rc=3). Those are the exits the original laps took. A lap that overruns
   its batch bound is not covered. `tools/batch_lap.py:348` `    for sig in (signal.SIGTERM, signal.SIGKILL):`
   with `:185` `KILL_GRACE_S = 5` ends every process of the lap within 5 s, while the trap's
   `lap_load_end` can need 45 s of wait plus its `rs 60` (`lap_load.sh:16`, `:42`).
   `tests/lap_load_end_lap.sh:64` (`timeout -k 5`) has the same limit. Such a lap still leaves its
   /run paths. The record's next-step sentence "with every exit removing its paths nothing these
   laps write accumulates" is therefore not proven, and the removal's `--why` and CHANGELOG entry
   must not claim "every exit". If overrun laps start leaving paths that accumulate, that is a new
   record.
2. **Configurations.** The noino site was verified only at 2/net/mesh/direct. Its 2/disk/caw/direct
   verify b20261024-06f62f is open (`data/defects.json:9068` `"state": "open",`) and is not relied
   on here. The ledger notes that already settled the 2/disk/caw/direct noino cells name the
   fenced-ledger batch b20261024-721cc7 (`data/criteria.runs.jsonl:16391` `... "row": "lap:lap_load_clean_noino" ... "why": "batch b20261024-721cc7: ...`).
   That attribution was wrong. The removal re-retires those cells as control FAILs of this cause,
   which is the correct disposition for them.
3. **Outside this record, not judged here.** In frc1-frc4, V's load logged about 1.8M failed
   operations per lap while the lap graded `stage=no-failstop`
   (`tests/evidence/20261024T164559Z_fledger_frc1/test1_load.txt:1`). That error volume is what
   filled /run. Whether those errors are correct behaviour for a fenced V is the fenced-reason lap's
   question, not this record's.
