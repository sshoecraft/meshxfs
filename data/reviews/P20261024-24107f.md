## Verdict: REJECT

The proposal's measurements are sound for what they cover:
- 10/10 fail on each control, with only the switch differing.
- N = 6 is correct.
- 6/6 PASS with exposure on candidate 0.90.134.

That coverage is narrower than the record and the incident.

1. The record states the general defect, and its own next step requires every harness run through `batch_lap.py` to be listed and covered (`data/defects.json:6593`). Only `tests/join_during_takeover.sh` prints `max_stall_ms=`. `depart_takeover_unmount` (`:161`, `:168`, `:178`) and `d_intents_2tcp_open_efi` (`:380`, run as `lap:openefi_img_gate_fault` without `--stall`) still time mounts and unmounts that the grader never sees. Removing the record as fixed would drop a live defect. Two things are missing:
   - the list the record asks for;
   - for each harness on it, either the same fix with its control and verification, or a record of its own that holds the remainder before this one is narrowed and removed.
2. The incident's path was the gate's fault lap (relay at `tests/capture_fault_gate.sh:186`). No lap ran that path: `tests/jdt_gate_stall_lap.py:111` runs `--healthy-only`. A fault-lap control and verification are needed, or the proposal must explain why the fault-lap call site is covered by measurement.
