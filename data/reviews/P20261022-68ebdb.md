## Verdict: REJECT

1. **The patch does not touch the stated cause.** The cause names `tests/fourkn_merge_2node.sh` and its 64 x 20 ms hold. That script is unchanged in the tree and in candidate 0.90.134. 3. **The proposal cites no instrumented evidence of the cause.** The record's evidence, read here, does cover the mechanism.

What is missing:
- **For the cause:** either `tests/fourkn_merge_2node.sh` fixed and covered by the verification, or a cause that names only what the patch changed. In the second case, the 2-node script's held window stays in the queue as an open defect.
- **For the control:** a control batch on build `16A5F5DDDB4852C9D5DB236` with the fix taken out, meaning the row as the fix found it: a held workload and no forced overlap. That can be the fix's diff reverted and recorded with its hash, or a knob setting that restores that workload. The control must reproduce VACUOUS.
- **For the fix-on batch:** a batch on the frozen candidate sized from that control's rate, with every lap PASS and `forced=landed`.
