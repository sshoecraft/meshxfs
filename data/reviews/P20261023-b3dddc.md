## Also not shown: the fix on more than two nodes

**The removal covers every configuration. The evidence covers one.**

- The record reaches every configuration: data/defects.json:3700 `      "nodes": 1,` and :3701 `      "config": "*/*/*",`. A removal disposes of it everywhere.
- Both batches ran at 2/net/mesh/direct, and the proposal argues the residual for two nodes only.

**On N nodes the proposal's own model gives a large residual.**

- The harness accepts a candidate only when H fires: tests/live_holder_wait.sh:402, above.
- It skips a candidate that a third node masters: :404 `    else loc=undetermined` and :407 `    [ "$loc" = "undetermined" ] && continue`.
- Under the proposal's model (dlm/dlm.c:2453), each shard is an H draw at 1/N, so the fixed harness aborts with probability (1 − 1/N)^16. In python3 that is 0.0100 at 4 nodes, 0.118 at 8, 0.356 at 16 and 0.602 at 32.
- This carries forward the proposal's unmeasured model. It is not a measurement.
- The harness is built to run inside larger clusters: tests/live_holder_wait.sh:216 `LHW_HOLD=${LHW_HOLD:-}` and :217 `vmhold_take "live_holder_wait $LABEL" "$H" "$W" ${LHW_HOLD//,/ } \`.

## What is missing

1. **A measurement of the shard-to-master link.** Either of these would do:
   - Probe, through the harness's own P7S-BAST-FIRE method, the master of every inode in two or more adjacent shards, on formations where those shards' masters differ. Show that the master changes at the shard boundary and only there.
   - Read the region's seed, page count and `hash_version` with `chk_mxfs -v`, compute each probed candidate's page with tests/tauth/pageof.c, and show that the sorted-active-list rule (page % N) predicts every measured master.
2. **A corrected cause.** The abort comes when every candidate's shard is mastered by W (test2, the requester), not by H.
3. **A verification that rests on that measurement.** With item 1 measured, the shard-count lap can stand as the reproducer. Without it, the verification has to run the harness's own search on fresh formations, with an exposure probe showing that the old candidates' shards were W-mastered on that formation.
4. **More than two nodes.** Either evidence that the fix ends the failure on the clusters the record reaches, or a basis for removing a record that reaches `*/*/*` on two-node evidence alone.

## Verdict

**REJECT.**

- **Check 1 is not met.**
  - The cause's central link, that eight consecutive candidates are one or two mastership draws, comes from the routing code. The proposal says so itself.
  - No cited run measures a master per shard, or the region's routing version.
  - The one cited mastership measurement, eight local results in two shards, fits other causes as well.
  - Both batches grade a constant-8 computation on inode numbers that never consults the module. They would have given the same 10 of 10 and 6 of 6 if inodes were not sharded at all.
  - The cause text says the abort comes when the draws land on H. The cited log (live_holder_wait_fault.log:5-12) and the harness's own rule (:402-403) show they landed on W.
- **Check 2 is met in form only.** The control reproduced the lap's arithmetic FAIL, never the record's abort. Its 10 of 10 is not the failure's rate.
- **Check 3 is met in form only.** The size is right and all six laps passed. The exposure probe is unconditional and shows no lap reached the defect's trigger.
- **Beyond two nodes**, a removal of a record that reaches every configuration rests on two-node evidence. On larger clusters the proposal's own model leaves the fixed harness aborting at (1 − 1/N)^16.
