## Other findings, not conditions of this kind

- **The `(gone)` line can be false.** It says only that the lap's original process group is empty (tools/batch_lap.py:182-189, :228). A lap whose harness launched anything under `timeout` without `--foreground`, `setsid`, or a daemonizing helper is logged `(gone)` while that work runs. The measurement above shows exactly this. Every capture gate entry runs that way (tests/capture_fault_gate.sh:157 and :173), so every `gate:<entry>` lap has this shape.
- **CHANGELOG.md:9 claims more than the fix does.** It opens `- **A batch lap that overruns its bound ends everything it started** (...)`. The measurement above contradicts that heading for a descendant running under `timeout`. The entry should state only what a verified fix does.
- **The observed run is not one of this record's cells.** `2/net/mesh/direct:gate:d_intents_2tcp_open_efi:2026-10-22T19:14:48Z` is not among them (data/defects.json:6908-6958 lists only the ten control laps), so removing the record would not settle that run either way.

## Verdict

**REJECT.**

- **Why.** A removal as fixed needs the cause proven by instrumented evidence, a fix that targets it, and a batch that exercises it.
  - The batches are sound, but only for a harness child that stays in the lap's process group: control 10 of 10 FAIL with exposure, N = 6 recomputed, 6 of 6 exposed PASS on the frozen candidate.
  - The record's only observed instance was a writer of a different kind. It was the gate entry that tests/capture_fault_gate.sh:173 runs under GNU `timeout`, which, as measured here, moves to a process group of its own.
  - Run on that launch, the fixed `run_bounded` logged `its process group 2894315 was ended (gone)` and returned 124, and the writer wrote 0.904 s later.
  - The defect the record describes ("a harness's own children outlive the lap: they go on writing after the lap is recorded") therefore still happens, in exactly the shape it was observed in.
- **What is missing, both:**
  1. **A fix** that ends every process the lap started, including descendants that left the lap's process group (`timeout` at tests/capture_fault_gate.sh:157/:173, `setsid`, daemonizing helpers). Its `gone` report must check those processes too, not only the original group.
  2. **A reproducer in the observed shape:** a bash harness that runs its writer under GNU `timeout` without `--foreground`, as tests/capture_fault_gate.sh:173 does. It needs a fix-off control that reproduces at a measured rate, and a fix-on batch sized from that control that passes every exposed lap.
