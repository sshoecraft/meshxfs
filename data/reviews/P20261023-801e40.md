## What the evidence does not cover (stated, not grounds for rejection)

- The proposal disclaims the nomark outputs (:2084 and the END clause of :2086), and they were
  never exercised: no lap log of either batch contains `nomark`. The lap grades a nomark entry
  ABORT (tests/census_scope_lap.sh:88 `if set(entries) != {"none"} or counted != len(nodes):`),
  so it could not have been counted as a PASS.
- The cited laps exercise only the case where the mark is present and no CCph line follows it.
  They do not show the scoped selector returning a marker the row did write. A selector that
  printed nothing at all would also produce `[none=2]`. The cause/why text in the change says the
  selection "reads only the CCph lines after the row's own kernel-log mark". That is a description
  of :2086. The laps measured the "only" half: the earlier line is excluded. They did not measure
  the "after the mark" half. An uncited lane on the same module does show that half:
  tests/evidence/lanes/g2_census_scope_precheck_20261023T181453Z/step6.log:6
  `LAP aw_census PASS: every node's rank left its phase: [fill=1,start=1]`, with the scoped
  selector in force, because step5.log:5 of that lane reads `last_phase_census[none=2]` and not
  the lap's planted marker. It is supporting only. The removal does not depend on it, and the
  removal claims nothing beyond the exclusion that the laps measured.
