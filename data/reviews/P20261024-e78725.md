## Verdict: REJECT

The narrowing from 1 to 32 nodes rests on one 32-node observation with no recorded cause
(`data/criteria.runs.jsonl:646`). The precondition it names ("a 32-member cluster") is that
observation restated. The supporting claims about what a 32-node prep does and how far it got
are inferred from the prep procedure and from elapsed time, not measured. No cited run shows any
precondition of this defect absent at 1..31 nodes, and the proposal says it does not claim one.
The field definition and the owner's "keep as is" ruling do not replace the runs that show the
precondition, which the gate requires (`docs/release-process.md:179`).

What would be needed: a recorded failure of this prep at 32/disk/caw/mpath with run.sh's prep
output and every node's kernel log, which identifies the failing step and cause. Then measured
evidence that this cause's precondition is absent at the excluded node counts. Alternatively,
the owner can rule explicitly that a single observation sets `nodes` without precondition
evidence, recorded where the gate reads it.
