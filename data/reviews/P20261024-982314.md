## Verdict: REJECT

The proposal proves 37 s of pool-lock contention from outside MXFS that preceded the failure. It
does not prove the two remaining conditions:

1. **No kernel logs.** No kernel log for test21-test28 covering the run (14:55:17Z-14:57:18Z) is
   cited or exists in the evidence directory, so "no MXFS signature" was never checked against
   evidence.
2. **The kill is not tied to the contention.** The 120 s kill landed about 8 s after
   `PREP STEP 6 end 6s` (prep.log:70), inside run.sh's post-prep path (host_release /
   marker_write / record, run.sh:2760-2765), which never logged completion. The proposal gives no
   measurement of what that path was doing or that it would have finished. "Would have fit
   without the waits" is inference.
