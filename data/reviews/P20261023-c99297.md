## Check 4. Fix-on batch sized from that control: arithmetic met, input not proven

Recomputed:
- The CDF of Beta(9, 2) is x^9 (10 - 9x). It equals 0.005 at L = 0.455713.
- N = ceil(ln 0.005 / ln(1 - L)) = ceil(8.710) = 9.
- (1 - L)^9 = 0.0042.

This matches the declared batch:
- `step25.log:1` `declared verification batch b20261022-548ef2 ... 9 clean laps (rate 9/10, lower bound 0.4557, delta 0.005, alpha 0.005)`

is lower and N larger. `tools/defects.py:900-902` says so: `With a probe, a FAIL counts only when the
probe saw the trigger: a FAIL the defect did not cause is an incident of its own, and counting it raises
the rate and shrinks the verification batch.`

## Verdict: REJECT

**Why.** The control did not show that it reproduced the failure it is meant to reproduce:
- All nine failing laps were killed by the gate's 228 s bound during the harness's sampling: rc=124,
  abort=0.
- None of them recorded an activation count, the harness's not-seen-in-flight abort or a kernel log of
  the pass.
- The claim that the pass had ended before the first sample in those laps is an inference, and the
  proposal's own lane file says so.
- The 9 of 10 rate that sized the 9-lap verification counts those laps.
- The removal would retire them, plus nine more that their own batch left unattributed.

**What is missing.** A fix-out control whose failing laps each show the cause in their own evidence.
Either of these would do:
- the lap's `P-TAUTH-PAGE-MINE ... via=takeover` lines from its A2 marker. These come from test1's
  `/tmp/jointk_dmesg.txt`, kept in the lap's evidence. They must end, with the pass's completion line,
  before the harness's first sample;
- the harness's own `takeover pass: ... in_flight=0` line and its
  `not seen in flight (activations=N; <summary>)` abort. The original sighting printed these under its
  longer bound.

That control must then be followed by a fix-on batch sized from it (`plan-verify --control <that batch>`).
The 172c3f failures need the same evidence before any removal retires them.
