There is no release pressure that the owner's absence would otherwise leave unresolved.
- **No owner direction is cited, and none was found.** A missing direction is not a direction, and an unattended run is not consent. The proposal swaps "the owner has not answered" for "the owner's question no longer applies". That is a conclusion drawn from silence.

The precedent of `D-TAUTH-MAKEFILE-DEFAULT-GOAL-IS-ONE-OBJECT-AND-CLEAN-MISSES-TWO-BINARIES` (`CHANGELOG.md:289`) does not apply. Its removal also rested on an edit of this Makefile (`TAUTH_GOAL_ALL`, `tests/tauth/Makefile:199`), but that record carried no hold for the owner. This record carries one. An earlier approval does not lift a condition that this record states for itself.
