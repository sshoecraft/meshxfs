## Verdict

**REJECT.** The cause is instrumented, and the fix is verified at 2/net/mesh/mpath and 4/net/mesh/mpath.
A removal disposes of the record everywhere, though, and the record was found at 2/disk/caw/mpath and
4/disk/caw/mpath. Its fix-on batches there did not pass every lap:

1. b20261024-408f0a: `5 PASS, 1 FAIL of 6 planned` (g2b_w12b_20261024T070007Z/step5.log:9).
2. b20261024-fd8125: `3 PASS, 1 FAIL of 6 planned` (g4c_w12b_20261024T070211Z/step18.log:7). Only 4 of its 6 laps ran.

The cited evidence for the 4/disk/caw/mpath control b20261024-239fb6 (step12.log:1 and step14.log:1)
is a refusal line, `is not this control (state)`, and shows none of its laps.

The record stays in the queue. Its verified lines for net/mesh stand. To remove it, both disk/caw
configurations need a fix-on batch of N=6 that passes every lap with exposure, sized from a control
whose laps are in the cited evidence. As the proposal itself anticipates, that may first require the
type-flip ESTALE record to be fixed. Until then the record remains open at disk/caw.
