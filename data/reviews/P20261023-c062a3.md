## Verdict: REJECT

- **Check 1: MET.** The mark-in-object instrument proves the mechanism: a plain `make` against these rules keeps all
  five objects compiled before a `pal/pal.h` change.
- **Check 2: MET.** 10 of 10 exposed control laps failed on the frozen tree, and the tree did not move.
- **Check 3: MET.** N = 6 is right for 10/10 at δ = α = 0.005. 6 of 6 laps passed with exposure.
- **Check 4: NOT MET.**
  - The record's defect is the Makefile's rules (`data/defects.json:6266`), and the patch leaves them unchanged.
    This proposal's own control measured them still stale on the candidate tree, 10 of 10.
  - The fix-on laps measure a path that does not use the Makefile.
  - `repeat_test.sh`, the Makefile's usage line, and the plain-make procedure the queue records still reach the
    defect.
  - The `make -B` sites never ran.
  - Removal on this evidence would close the record because the defect is covered by a workaround in five scripts.
    `CLAUDE.md:229-231` and `:235-238` rule that out.

What would satisfy this check:
- A change at the cause the record names. Each object rule in `tests/tauth/Makefile` must rebuild when any header its
  source includes changes; compiler-generated dependency files are one way. Then a plain `make` in `tests/tauth`
  cannot link an object older than a header it includes, whoever runs it.
- A control batch with that change taken out (a `--revert` diff or the change's own knob). Then a verification batch
  sized from it, on the candidate.
- Both batches must use a lap that builds through the Makefile with a plain `make`:
  - build;
  - change a header each rule omits today (`pal/pal.h` for all five, `dlm/dlm.h` for dlm_shared.o, tauth_store.o and
    tauth_ledger.o);
  - plain `make` again;
  - require the mark in every object and in a binary linked from them (for example `dlm_ledger_test`).
- If the helper and `make -B` stay, they must either be shown unnecessary once the Makefile is fixed or be measured
  too. Today no run exercises `make -B`.
- The record stays in the queue until then. Its impact, reach and evidence must not be restated to fit the five
  scripts' bypass.
