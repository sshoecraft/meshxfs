## Check 1: the cause is proven by instrumented evidence, not inference. MET

**The hypothesis.**

- The record states it at data/defects.json:5842: `      "summary": "scripts/rig_lane.sh keeps each configuration a lane holds with a holder process (tail --pid=<lane> -f /dev/null) that notices the lane has exited only at its next 1 s poll, so for up to a second after a lane ends its configurations stay held, and a lane started in that second on one of them is refused (rc 3) as if the ended lane still ran.",`
- The mechanism in the tree:
  - The lane takes the lock: scripts/rig_lane.sh:70-71 `    exec {fd}>>"$lock"` / `    if ! flock -n "$fd"; then`.
  - It writes its owner record: :76 `    echo "$$ $(date -u +%FT%TZ) lane $NAME" > "$lock"`.
  - It hands the descriptor to a holder: :77 `    tail --pid=$$ -f /dev/null < /dev/null > /dev/null 2>&1 &`.
  - It closes its own copy before any step runs: :79 `    exec {fd}>&-`. Steps start later, at :93 `    bash -c "$line" < /dev/null > "$OUT/step$n.log" 2>&1`.
  - So once the lane has exited, only its holder can still hold the lock.
- The fix, and its switch:
  - :57 `HOLDERS=()` and :78 `    HOLDERS+=("$!")` record each holder.
  - :58-66 end them on exit: `release_holds() {` / `    [ "${LANE_HOLD_RELEASE:-1}" = 0 ] && return` / `    local pid` / `    for pid in "${HOLDERS[@]}"; do` / `        kill "$pid" 2>/dev/null` / `        wait "$pid" 2>/dev/null` / `    done` / `}` / `trap release_holds EXIT`.
  - With LANE_HOLD_RELEASE=0, :59 returns before any kill. That leaves only the holder's poll, which is the behaviour before the fix.
