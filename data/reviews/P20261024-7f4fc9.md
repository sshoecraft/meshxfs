## Check 1: the cause is proven by instrumented evidence, not inference

The proposal's cause (proposal `change.cause`) reads: "tools/defects.py cmd_measure required its PASS and FAIL laps alone to equal the plan while check_batch_lap counts every recorded lap against it ... the fix counts each settled INCOMPLETE or INFRA lap toward the plan (:1078-1088)".

What is measured:
- The original sighting. tests/evidence/lanes/g8b_a17b_20261024T111854Z/step2.log:5
  `defects: batch b20261024-1d2fde planned 10 laps and has 9; run the rest first`
  and :16 `control b20261024-1d2fde: 9 of 10 laps failed with the fix out (0 run by this call)`.
- The control reproduces that refusal on the same live batch, and the lap measures the precondition (9 measured + 1 settled):
  tests/evidence/batch_laps/b20261024-7d38ae/20261024T125017Z-13c3d482.log:5
  `EXPOSED: batch b20261024-1d2fde of D-SURVIVOR-DECLARES-A-PEER-DEAD-ON-ITS-OWN-WINDOW-INSIDE-THE-PEERS-LONGER-LEASE (lap:peer_window at 8/disk/caw/mpath) holds 9 measured and 1 settled lap(s) of 10 planned`
  :6 `  measure rc=1 state=open rates=0: defects: batch b20261024-1d2fde planned 10 laps and has 9 (0 settled); run the rest first`
  The other nine control logs have the same lines 3-7.

For a settled **INCOMPLETE** lap, that evidence is direct. The measure refusal is measured, and the knob that brings it back is the one the fix adds.

The cause text is wrong about the code it names, though, and the fix's INFRA arm rests on that wrong premise:
- tools/defects.py:1016 `    laps = [r for r in batch_runs(batch) if r.get("outcome") in ("PASS", "FAIL", "INCOMPLETE")]`
- tools/defects.py:1020 `    if len(laps) >= int(batch["planned"]):`
  check_batch_lap does not count "every recorded lap". It counts PASS, FAIL and INCOMPLETE, and an INFRA lap does not use up a planned lap. The record's own summary says so: data/defects.json:8614 "check_batch_lap counts PASS, FAIL and INCOMPLETE laps against the plan".
- run.sh:712 `    batch_out=$(python3 "$REPO/tools/defects.py" batch-check "$BATCH" --row "$BATCH_ROW" --at "$CONFIG" \`
  run.sh runs that check before every lap. INFRA is an outcome run.sh records (run.sh:1944 `            *"{EXT4-ROOTDISK}"*) pa_outcome=INFRA ;;`, :2307 `        agg=INFRA`, and tools/criteria.py:258/:272/:278).
- The fix: tools/defects.py:1083-1084
  `    settled = [] if os.environ.get("MXFS_MEASURE_COUNT_SETTLED", "1") == "0" else \`
  `        [r for r in laps if r.get("outcome") in ("INCOMPLETE", "INFRA") and r not in unresolved]`
  and :1086 `    if measured + len(settled) != int(batch["planned"]):`

Read together, these lines give a case the proposal never exercised. A batch planned at 10 records an INFRA lap. check_batch_lap does not count it (:1016), so run.sh accepts 10 more PASS/FAIL laps. The INFRA lap is then settled by a reviewed infra-proven. Before the fix, measure closed this batch, because 10 PASS/FAIL equals the plan. With the fix, measure counts 10 + 1 settled = 11 != 10 and refuses with "planned 10 laps and has 11 (1 settled); run the rest first". No lap can be added and none removed. That is the same stuck state the record describes, now reached through INFRA. So the fix does not make measure and check_batch_lap count the same laps. It moves the disagreement from settled INCOMPLETE to settled INFRA. The record's `next` (data/defects.json:8616) also says "tools/harness_batch.py's resume count (args.runs = planned - every recorded lap) agrees with check_batch_lap". That is untrue for INFRA: tools/harness_batch.py:303-306 counts every recorded lap of the batch, INFRA included, and check_batch_lap (:1016) does not.

I found this by reading the code. It was not measured, so the INFRA case is unproven in either direction. That is the point: the burden is on the proposal. Its cause statement contradicts tools/defects.py:1016, and it claims a fix for "each settled INCOMPLETE or INFRA lap" while no lap of either batch held an INFRA lap (every EXPOSED line names "1 settled" on b20261024-1d2fde and b20261024-bda0e1, which the record calls INCOMPLETE). Not met for the cause as stated.

## Verdict: REJECT

The INCOMPLETE case is reproduced and verified. The control refused 10 of 10 with the exact refusal of the original sighting, and the fix-on batch, correctly sized at 6 from 10/10, passed 6 of 6 with exposure. The removal still cannot be approved, because the cause is misstated and the fix goes beyond what was verified:

1. The cause says check_batch_lap "counts every recorded lap against" the plan. tools/defects.py:1016 counts PASS, FAIL and INCOMPLETE only. INFRA laps do not use up a planned lap, and run.sh:712 checks that before every lap.
2. The fix counts settled INFRA laps toward the plan (tools/defects.py:1083-1084, :1086). By the code at :1016/:1020, a batch with its full plan of PASS/FAIL laps plus one settled INFRA lap was measurable before the fix and is refused for good after it (measured + settled > planned). measure and check_batch_lap still count different laps. The difference has moved to INFRA, and tools/harness_batch.py:303-306 counts INFRA differently from check_batch_lap as well. No lap exercised this. The probe as written (tests/measure_settled_lap.py:117) cannot select such a batch.

What would make it approvable:
- Correct the cause to what tools/defects.py:1016 does.
- Make measure, check_batch_lap and harness_batch's resume count the same laps toward the plan. Either count only settled INCOMPLETE in measure, or count INFRA in all three. This is one change, stated as one.
- Extend the lap, or add one, that also builds the settled-INFRA case in its scratch tree. That includes an INFRA lap past a full plan of PASS/FAIL laps, since run.sh's batch-check allows it. Then run a control and a sized fix-on batch that show both cases close, or show by measurement that a batch lap can never record INFRA.

The record stays in the queue.
