## Verdict

**REJECT.**

The three remove-fixed conditions are proven by measured evidence:
- The control and the fix-on batch ran on the same frozen 0.90.134 tree, with only the switch changed.
- With the switch off, 10 of 10 laps failed with the record's signature. The phase census and the rank records place each rank in its wait.
- With the switch on, 6 of 6 laps passed with exposure, and N = 6 recomputes from delta = alpha = 0.005 on k = n = 10.

The removal is rejected for what it would settle:
- Applied, it writes `retired_by` (fixed) on 2/net/mesh/drbd:alloc_witness:2026-10-21T22:35:00Z.
- By the proposal's own account, that run fails with this fix in.
- Its failure belongs to the open major record D-2NETMESH-ALLOC-WITNESS-RANK-FILL-CARVED-NOTHING-IN-ITS-PARTITION.
- The proposal's premise that the run stays accounted for by the two open records is contradicted by tools/criteria.py:777-779, which drops a settled FAIL before it consults the records that name it.

A removal can be approved on this evidence only once it retires no run that this fix does not end.
