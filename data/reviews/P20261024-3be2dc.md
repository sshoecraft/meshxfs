## Check 1: the cause is proven by instrumented evidence, not inference

**The code.** In the control branch the offset is one ssh call's midpoint. In the fixed branch it is the minimum-RTT midpoint over an open session. The oracle maps each host-logged gap login onto X's clock with that offset.

- `tests/scst_pr_abort_shutdown_race.sh:402` `h0=$(date +%s%3N); n0=$(rsh $X "date +%s%3N" | tail -1 | tr -dc 0-9); h1=$(date +%s%3N)`
- `:403` `off=$(( n0 - (h0 + h1) / 2 )); rtt=$(( h1 - h0 ))`
- `:405` `coproc CLK { timeout 30 $SSH "$X" 'echo ready; while read -r l; do date +%s%3N; done' 2>/dev/null; }`
- `:412-413` keep the shortest of five round trips: `rtt=$(( h1 - h0 )); off=$(( n0 - (h0 + h1) / 2 ))`
- `:387` `c=$(( $(date -d "$t" +%s%3N) + off ))`
- `:388` `n=$(( n + $(awk -v c="$c" '$1 < c && $2 > c' "$OUT/writes.log" | wc -l) ))`

**The measured offsets.** With the fix out, every control lap read an offset of about rtt/2.

- `tests/evidence/batch_laps/b20261024-dad6d2/20261024T143459Z-330ac3eb.log:15` `X's clock - this host's: 248 ms (round trip 512 ms)`
- The other nine control laps, each at `:15`, read 266/546, 299/611, 234/484, 245/505, 204/423, 208/431, 212/440, 210/428 and 210/421 ms (offset/round trip).

With the fix in, minutes later on the same X, every lap read an offset of 7 to 10 ms with round trips of 3 to 8 ms.

- `tests/evidence/batch_laps/b20261024-b07bc3/20261024T152002Z-41ae6869.log:15` `X's clock - this host's: 7 ms (round trip 3 ms)`
- `20261024T152524Z-de197789.log:15` `X's clock - this host's: 9 ms (round trip 3 ms)`

So the single-call midpoint was 200 to 290 ms high. Its error bound is rtt/2, and the open-session error bound is 4 ms or less.

## Verdict: APPROVE

1. The cause is proven from instrumented data. The single-call midpoint offset was 204 to 299 ms high in all ten control laps, against 7 to 10 ms over the open session. Applying the oracle's own predicate to the recorded write and login timestamps of each failing control lap turns 0 spanning writes into 4 to 7 once the offset is corrected. The two control PASSes were false exposures, writes issued after the login.
2. The fix-off control failed 8 of 10 exposed laps, which is its measured rate. Its inputs were unchanged, and its ledger rows match.
3. The fix-on batch was sized from that control (13 laps, recomputed) and declared before its first lap. It ran the frozen file carrying the fix (hash matched), and all 13 laps passed. Each lap's exposure is a genuine write blocked about 4 s and carried across a gap login.

This is a defect in the test's oracle, configuration-independent and host-side. The removal disposes of the record as FIXED AND VERIFIED under the stated verification at 2/disk/caw/mpath on candidate 0.90.134. It makes no claim about MXFS behaviour.
