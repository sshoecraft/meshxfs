## Verdict: REJECT

Checks 1 through 4 hold for the single-path bind at 2/net/mesh/direct. The removal reaches further than that. The record names the multipath login as a defect site, and the patch changes both the multipath and the `--also` logins (`tools/lun_pool.sh:421`, `:357`). Every */*/mpath configuration binds through the multipath login (`run.sh:439`), and its shutdown path differs from the one measured (multipathd, `replacement_timeout 5`, `no_path_retry 4`). No control, no fix-on lap and no instrument has run either login. Removing the record would declare them fixed and verified on inference.

The smallest thing that unblocks this is one of the following.

1. Run a two-path variant of the reproducer (`tests/lun_pool_shutdown_lap.sh` binding with `--paths 2`) at an mpath configuration such as 2/net/mesh/mpath, with exposure taken on that variant:
   - first a control with the fix reverted;
   - then a fix-on batch on the candidate, sized from that control's own rate, or from a `--transfer` whose reason can be checked;
   - then the same for an `--also` bind on a node whose first bind followed a recordless boot.
2. Or move the multipath and `--also` sites into a record of their own, with its own reproducer, control and fix-on batch, and resubmit this removal for the single-path bind only.

Either way, the resubmission must cite the instrumented chain (the three `tests/evidence/20261022T15*_fourkn_shutdown_bare` directories, or an equivalent capture of the pool LUN's shutdown journal). The evidence it cites now shows the toggle and its outcome, not the cause.
