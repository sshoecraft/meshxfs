## Verdict: REJECT

The in-process evidence is sound. It shows that the three entry points wrote after the node's authority closed and that the gates stop them there. It does not prove the removal:

1. **The recorded failure was never reproduced or shown gone.** The harness's store never refuses a write, so neither batch had a refused publish, an uncertain page, a reconcile, or a rate. No run with the fix in exists where the target refuses the fenced node.
2. **The rig cause is partly inferred.** 28,034 of 31,510 refused publishes are measured as FROZEN activations. That count comes from the log's suppression totals, which the proposal does not use. The remaining ~3,476 (~11%) are assigned to `dlm_page_ensure_mine` by reading code, on a path that logs nothing when its write fails.
3. **The removal would retire the rig run as fixed** although its survivor stall (75 s) and its rejoin mount timeout are not shown to be this defect's. By the fix's own comments, requests on the fenced node's pages still park while the view names it.

What a resubmission needs:
- A run of the recorded failure on the candidate's module against a target that refuses the fenced node, with the fix out and with it in. The record's own step is the template: a 2-node TCP pair, one node's filesystem shut down while it holds ledger pages, its key preempted. Count P-TAUTH-PUBLISH-FAIL and the target's conflicts per second after P290-AUTH-CLOSED. With the fix out it must reproduce the flood. With the fix in it must show none, and show `P-TAUTH-PAGE-AUTH-CLOSED`/`P-TAUTH-HANDOFF-AUTH-CLOSED` engaging. Laps of a harness whose store refuses a closed node's writes the way the target does would also reproduce the loop with the fix out, and can serve as the control and fix-on batches.
- Instrumented attribution of every refused publish in the fix-out run to a gated entry point, including a probe on the activation that `dlm_page_acquire` makes for `dlm_page_ensure_mine` (`dlm/dlm.c:4629`), so no share of the flood is assigned by inference.
- For the cell 8/net/mesh/mpath:path_peer_withdrawn:2026-10-22T19:58:42Z: measured evidence that its survivor stall (max_stall_ms=75266) and test28's MOUNT_RC=124 were this defect and end with the fix. Otherwise those failures must be recorded under an open record that names their cause before a removal retires the run as fixed.
