## 1. What the record reports, and what a disproval has to show

The record's summary (data/defects.json:5946 entry, as printed by `proposal show`): `During a foreign replay the surviving node writes allocation-group header blocks (AGF) without holding that group's lock: PROBE-A AG-META-WRITE-NOT-HELD fires 4-10 times in every lap ... the probe exists because such a write can erase a live peer's change to the header, which no record has shown cannot happen here`.

The record says what disposes of it (data/defects.json:5953): `This is a code read, not a measurement: the record is disposed of only by the 3-node lap (a live peer allocating in the replayed AG while a third node's death is replayed, the peer's acquire timed against P163-RECOVERY-COMPLETE, the AG headers checked on every node and by chk_mxfs), and the detector then tells replay writes apart (b_mxfs_recov_image and the replay task) instead of firing on them.` The same line also holds: `... then propose remove --disproved citing each lap's ordering and chk_mxfs, and the detector change (PROBE-A not firing on a recov=1 image written by the replay task) as its own fix.`

A disproval has to show two things. First, that no live peer's change to a replayed AG can be erased by the replay's write. Second, that the probe's firing, which is the observed behaviour, is not an MXFS defect.

## 3. The lap's ordering does not show the exclusion the disproval depends on (gap)

The proposal's central claim is `every live change to a replayed AG waited for the replayer's ladder entry`. Its argument (2) attributes that wait to the frozen grant on the replayed AG. It does not measure when that truncate acquired the AG or changed its header. It also grades only truncates in a replayed AG (:269-272).

The truncates in AGs the replay never wrote waited exactly as long as the exposed ones:

- Lap frx18t3. tests/evidence/20261024T125710Z_fragag_frx18t3/V_extents.txt:12 `EXT 5 5` puts f5 wholly in AG 5. Every truncate in every lap stalled 16-31 s (`max_stall_ms` at line 24 of each log: 16487 to 30872). Truncates in AGs the replay did not touch were held to the same instant. The measured ordering therefore cannot tell these two causes apart:

- the per-AG exclusion that argument (2) describes;
- a stall that holds every survivor operation through the recovery. That stall is an open critical defect, and fixing it would remove it.

The victim may also have cached AGs 5 and 7. Nothing in the lap measures that either way. The mechanism in argument (2) therefore remains a code read: xfs/xfs_log_recover.c:3484 `mxfs_shadow_eval_token(`, dlm/v5_mount.c:14083 `static void v5_tcp_declare_dead(...)`, :18480 `static int v5_dead_grants_retire(...)`, :19004 `rc = mxfs_dlm_caw_purge_node(ctx->dlm_caw, (uint8_t)dead_slot);`. The record asked for `the peer's acquire timed against P163-RECOVERY-COMPLETE` and `the AG headers checked on every node`. Neither was measured. The headers were checked only by the offline chk_mxfs, after every node had unmounted.

Missing: a measurement that ties a live peer's wait to the replayed AG's grant and not to the general stall. Two forms would do: the peer's AG acquire timestamped by the module and set against the replay's last header write and against v5_dead_grants_retire or mxfs_dlm_caw_purge_node; or a control where a live peer's operation in an AG the victim did not hold completes during the replay while the replayed-AG operation does not.

## 4. The observed behaviour is MXFS code firing, and its fix is neither in the tree nor in the queue (gap)

The behaviour the record was filed on is PROBE-A firing, with a once-per-boot stack dump, on every foreign replay. That is MXFS code: pal/linux/xfs_buf.c:12006 `		if (!held) {`, :12010 `			mxfs_probe_ratelimited("mxfs: PROBE-A AG-META-WRITE-NOT-HELD agno=%u ...recov=%d\n",`, :12022-12023 `if (atomic_cmpxchg(&pa_dumped, 0, 1) == 0)` / `mxfs_probe_stack();`. The probe's own comment (:11991-11993) records that this dump tripped soak's kernel-log scan: `while the once-per-boot dump_stack's "Call Trace:" line tripped soak's kernel-log scan`. The probe now prints `recov=` (:12021 `bp->b_flags, bp->b_mxfs_recov_image);`) but still fires on recov=1. The gate at :12006 tests only `held`.

The proposal says: `The detector's inability to tell a replay write from an unauthorised one (recov=1) is a detector change, not this record.` That change is not in the tree (above). No queue record holds it. Of the 277 records in data/defects.json, two mention `AG-META-WRITE-NOT-HELD`: this one, and D-8NETMESH-SURVIVOR-SHUTDOWN-FALSE-FRESH-AG-GRANT-AFTER-TAKEOVER. The second only cites a PROBE-A line in a shutdown (`780980 P218-WRITE-REFUSED with PROBE-A AG-META-WRITE-NOT-HELD agno=7`) and is not the detector change. If this record is removed as disproved, a false-firing MXFS detector that dumps a stack on every foreign replay stays in the module, and nothing in the queue accounts for it. A detector that fires on correct behaviour is a detector defect. That calls for the detector-defect disposition, with its fix in the tree and the row passing since. "Not an MXFS defect" does not describe it. The record's own plan (data/defects.json:5953) pairs the disproval with `the detector change ... as its own fix`.

Missing: the PROBE-A change (no fire, or a separate non-alarm line, for a recov=1 image written by the replay task) in the tree with its diff named, or a queue record carrying it.

## Verdict: REJECT

The 20 laps show that in this lap shape, on candidate build 8DBA16AEB7FDD3066C9C1BC, no live truncate in a replayed AG completed before the replayer's ladder entry, and chk_mxfs found the volume clean every time. That is a clean result. It does not prove what this disposition claims:

1. The ordering does not show the exclusion. Truncates in AGs the replay never wrote (frx18t10 f16 and f37 in AG 7, frx18t3 f5 in AG 5) completed in the same window as the exposed ones, after a 16-31 s stall that an open critical record names. The lap times syscall completion, not the AG acquire or the header change. The mechanism argument (2) depends on therefore remains a code read, and the record (data/defects.json:5953) says a code read does not dispose of it.
2. The observed behaviour, PROBE-A firing and dumping a stack on every foreign replay, is MXFS's own detector misfiring (pal/linux/xfs_buf.c:12006-12023). The proposal calls it a needed detector change. That change is not in the tree, and no queue record carries it. "Not an MXFS defect" does not describe it.
3. The crash_audit run of 2026-10-10 ran on build B3240CC63168EA159224A2C, with a 72 s replay and two live peers, and its AG headers were never audited (chk_test3.out:1-2). The removal would settle it on evidence from a different build.

Needed before a disproval can be approved:

- A measurement that ties a live peer's wait to the replayed AG's grant: the module-stamped acquire, against the replay's last header write and the grant retire, or a control showing that a non-held AG's operation proceeds during the replay while the held one waits.
- The PROBE-A fix in the tree with its diff named, or a queue record holding it.
- For the 2026-10-10 run: evidence that its build carried the exclusion, or this run's cell left on the record (or another record) rather than settled by this removal.
