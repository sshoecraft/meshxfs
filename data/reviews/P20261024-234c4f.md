## 4. What the removal also disposes of, and why it must not

The record's `next` field carries open work on the fix's own safety, and no other queue entry carries it:

- `data/defects.json:7207` (this record's `next`): `... the residual hazard is the late REGISTER itself, closed only by creating no registration once the incarnation is fenceable, an unresolved REGISTER keeping the fence from certifying, or a fence that aborts the tasks of every nexus of the LU.` The same field also says `... measure B's pieces (register_ms, when the timeout fires on a path held unanswering) from the new log line.`
- A scan of every record in `data/defects.json` for `late REGISTER|delayed REGISTER|timed-out REGISTER|unresolved REGISTER|FILL-LATE|PATH-QUARANTINED` finds this record and no other. `D-2NETMESH-PATH-FLAP-12S-WINDOW-STALLS-120S-ACKED-FILES-UNREAD` mentions only `pr_fill`/`late-path fill`. `D-FENCED-VICTIM-MAY-REREGISTER` asserts the opposite premise, `MXFS itself registers only at mount`, and does not name the fill.

The proposal cites a ruling, and that ruling says the fix's admission rests on a bound that has
not been established:

- `docs/rulings/silent-peer-death-under-the-stall-limit.md:238-240` `What has to be bounded is B, the real time from the fill's admission until its REGISTER has either completed or can no longer execute at the target (queueing, the command timeout T, and a confirmed cancellation, not merely an ABORT TASK sent).`
- `:247-248` `If cancellation has no enforced upper bound, no finite threshold proves the absence of a registration after the fence, ...`
- `:259-260` `Measured maxima validate an enforced bound; they do not establish one.`
- `:305-306` `Taking the path offline at the initiator keeps the node's own later writes off it, but not a write already queued at the target behind the delayed REGISTER.`

The fix as built makes no confirmed cancellation. It bounds the REGISTER by its initiator-side
timeout and, when the outcome is unknown, unregisters and takes the path offline:
`pal/linux/kern.c:7214` `if (now + MXFS_PR_PATH_TIMEOUT_MS + MXFS_PR_FILL_MARGIN_MS >=`, then `:7230-7235`
`if (fence_ms && MXFS_PR_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN(r)) {` ... `mxfs_pr_path_quarantine(...)`, and `:7093`
`rc = scsi_device_set_state(p, SDEV_OFFLINE);`. That is the offline-only quarantine that `:305-306` says does
not close the hazard. The proposal itself concedes the hazard is unmeasured: `no lap measured a REGISTER
answering after its bound`. Its sentence `The residual hazard ... is what the fix's admission bound and
quarantine address` is the proposer's inference, and the ruling it cites contradicts it.

This hazard is a credible data-integrity defect: a fenced node's key re-registered on a nexus, with
writes queued behind it. Under the 12 s default window the fill now runs routinely, where before it
never ran at that window. Removing this record would delete the only queue entry holding that open
item and its measurement step, and would publish the late-path fill as fixed and verified while its
own soundness condition is open. A removal must not drop open work this way.

## Verdict

**REJECT.**

The observed failure has a proven cause, its controls reproduced, and the fix-on batches passed
(checks 1-3 are met). But a removal disposes of the whole record, and this record's `next` is the
only place in the queue that tracks the late-REGISTER fencing hazard of the very code path the fix
turns on (`data/defects.json:7207`). The ruling the proposal cites says that hazard is not closed by
a timeout-bounded admission with an offline quarantine (`docs/rulings/silent-peer-death-under-the-stall-limit.md:238-240`, `:247-248`, `:305-306`).
The proposal admits it was not measured.

Smallest thing that unblocks this removal: open the late-REGISTER hazard as its own record. It needs
the ruling's lines as evidence and the measurement of B on a path held unanswering as its next step,
at 2/disk/caw/mpath on the 12 s window, and the record must be in the queue before or with this
removal. Then propose the removal again, citing that record. The alternative is to close the hazard
first by measurement and an enforced bound, which means confirmed cancellation, a fence that will
not certify while a REGISTER is unresolved, or a fence that aborts every nexus. On a resubmission,
also keep the fix-on laps' kernel logs (`PF_KEEP_KMSG=1`), so that the absence of
`P-PR-PATH-FILL-LATE` and `P-PR-PATH-QUARANTINED` in them can be read.
