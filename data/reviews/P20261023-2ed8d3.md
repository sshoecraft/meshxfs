## Verdict

**REJECT.**

**The three remove-fixed checks hold at 2/net/mesh/direct on candidate 0.90.134:**
- **The cause.** An instrumented pre-fix build measured it: P-RELALL-WALL 27939 and 28708 ms for about 32000 grants and 3383-3684 ms for 4001. The umount task's stacks place it in a page commit per grant inside dlm_wire_release_all.
- **The control.** The knob control reproduced in 10 of 10 exposed laps on the frozen build. With every grant locally mastered, `dl_relall_threads=1` runs the same serial loop the pre-fix build ran, at the same 0.9 ms a grant.
- **The fix-on batch.** Sized N = 6 from that control (recomputed), it passed 6 of 6 exposed laps, with A's unmount at 10256-12906 ms.

**The removal is rejected for what it would dispose of:**
- **Scope.** The record reaches every configuration of two nodes or more, and only 2/net/mesh/direct was run. The case for the rest is code reading for disk/caw and an unmeasured claim for net/mesh, that the wall is "not per node or attachment". The queue's own records contradict that claim, measuring the per-commit cost on 2/net/mesh/drbd at roughly ten times this configuration's. The fix divides the wall by about 4.2 and leaves it proportional to the grants held times that cost. Nothing measured shows the last member's unmount under `stall_limit_s` where commits cost more.
- **The cause text.** It says unmounts of 28-61 s, all over the limit. 28 s was the release-all, and three serial unmounts at 32000 grants measured under 30 s.

**The record stays open with its applied verification**, which already stops it blocking 2/net/mesh/direct on 0.90.134.
