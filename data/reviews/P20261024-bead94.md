## Verdict: REJECT

The narrowing to `*/*/direct4k` (and `nodes 2`) rests only on reading `tests/fourkn_merge_2node.sh:107/:159`
and `tests/merge_overrun_lap.sh`. No run at an excluded configuration shows the defect's precondition
absent there. The measured record shows the opposite: `fourkn_merge_2node.sh` labels its runs from
`MXFS_CONFIG`, which defaults to `2/net/mesh/direct` (`:108`), and seven laps ran their workload under
`2/disk/caw/direct` and `2/net/mesh/direct` (2026-10-22). The defect's trigger is a host-side overrun,
and nothing about configuration excludes it. To narrow, the proposal needs measured evidence that the
client-only-bounded worker cannot run at each excluded configuration. None of the cited evidence
supplies that.
