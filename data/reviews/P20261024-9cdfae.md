## Check 1: the cause is proven by instrumented evidence, not inference

What the proposal cites for the cause: a source comment, not a measurement.
xfs/xfs_mxfs_relbar.c:903-910

`* (D-CAW-MPATH-8NODE-REUSED-INODE-NAMED-BY-TWO-DIRENTS-NLINK-1-AFTER-SUITE).`

volume read from the 2026-10-19 runs. The argument's other support is the CHANGELOG paragraph of
a removal that went through no review: data/reviews/ holds no review that names

## Verdict

**REJECT.** A remove --fixed needs the cause proven by instrumented evidence, and the proposal
does not provide it.

1. The proposal's only cited evidence for the 2026-10-19 trace is a source comment
   (xfs/xfs_mxfs_relbar.c:903-910) plus the CHANGELOG text of a removal no reviewer ever checked.
   The uncited kernel logs prove a PR holder re-logged a stale LOCK core naming `test28`
   (path_flap kmsg_test23_fromnode.gz:171223-171287; g4b kmsg_test20_fromnode.gz:150063-150064).
   Nothing measured ties that to inode 13147165 or 6872560 being named twice:
   - no chk log names the entries;
   - no image was kept;
   - no line gives the target number of the resurrected entry;
   - 13147165's earlier life as a directory (kmsg_test28_fromnode.gz:125249;
     kernlog_test27.gz:140806) is unaccounted for.
2. The control forces the re-log arm on a core that matches the disk and grades only the probe
   the knob selects. Data stayed correct with the fix out (`LS=b1 b2`). It did not reproduce the
   defect, and the record's acceptance criterion (chk_clean after path_answer_lost) was never
   run fix-off.
3. Nothing at 8/disk/caw/mpath verifies the record's 8-node corruption.

What would carry this removal:
- Instrumented evidence linking the double name to the shared-holder re-log. For example, a
  fix-off run (relog_auth_granted_mode=0) of path_answer_lost + chk_clean, or a lap built to
  leave a stale entry, whose CORRUPT volume is read before remount and shows that the second
  entry is the one the PR holder's re-log brought back.
- A fix-off control under the record's own criterion, giving a measured rate.
- A fix-on batch sized from that rate at both 4/ and 8/disk/caw/mpath.
