## Verdict: REJECT

- **What is met.**
  - The run_none cause is measured.
  - Its knob-off control reproduced 10 of 10 on the intact frozen tree.
  - The fix-on batch was sized correctly from that control (N = 6, recomputed) and passed 6 of 6, each lap exposed. Both logs with fsx's usage text are in each run's evidence.
- **What is not met.** The record (`data/defects.json:5725-5726`) names a second cause, fsx.sh sending fsx's output to `/tmp/fsx.$$.*.log` and deleting it in its EXIT trap, and the fsx.sh part of the fix that answers it.
  - That cause is supported only by a reading of the old code.
  - Every control lap ran the fixed fsx.sh, so no control has taken that part of the fix out (`docs/release-process.md:160-162`, `:166`).
  - The control is therefore not "the fix taken out" for this record. Nothing measured shows that the fsx.sh part was needed, or that it is what ended its half of the defect.
  - Both earlier reviews raised this (`P20261022-fa5891.md:226`, `P20261023-8efcf4.md:259-264`). The proposal answers neither.

**What is missing.** One of these:

- **Either:** a control with the record's whole fix taken out, then a fix-on batch sized from it:
  - fsx.sh as it was before the fix: writing `/tmp/fsx.$$.*.log` and deleting it in its EXIT trap. Run it as the candidate's source with only that change reverted, recorded as a variant with the reverted diff's sha256.
  - Run it through run.sh at the configuration, with the fsx checks forced to fail.
  - Give it a probe that does not need the fixed fsx.sh to name its log. This lap ABORTs without that name (`tests/fsx_log_pull_lap.sh:60-61`).
  - Measure it losing the log.
  - Then run a verification batch on the current candidate attempt, sized from that control's rate. Each lap must show both `<node>_fsx.<seed>.<ops>.log` files in `tests/evidence/run_fsx_<run id>/`.
- **Or:** this record carries only the cause this control measured (run_none pulled nothing for a row that did not pass). The fsx.sh EXIT-trap deletion then goes to a record of its own, which stays open until a control and a verification of its own dispose of it.

Either way, the resubmission must also close observation 1 (the run.sh the laps ran against the run.sh in the tree).
