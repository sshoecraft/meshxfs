- Verdict: **REJECT**

The kind requires two things: (1) positive evidence of the infrastructure fault, from outside MXFS, that precedes the failure; (2) no MXFS signature in the run's kernel logs. Absence of a signature alone is not proof. Check 2 cannot be performed because the run has no kernel log at all. Check 1 is partly met: the host disk was saturated, and that is measured. What links the saturation to this run's two failures is inferred from per-minute averages and never measured. The proposal also misstates the lap's own assertions.
