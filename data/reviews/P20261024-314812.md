## Verdict: APPROVE

The reported behaviour is the lap's rmmod refused with "Module mxfs is in use", so insmod
fails with "File exists", before any MXFS workload runs. That is true of all 46 cells (each one
ledger run, each log with the abort and no workload). Direct evidence shows the cause is the
lane's queue and not MXFS. The prep step left mxfs mounted on both nodes (step2.log). The lap
unmounts only /mnt/fourkn before rmmod (fourkn_merge_2node.sh:158). After the same prep on the
same pairs and build, unmounting /mnt/shared made rmmod succeed (mxfs_loaded=0 on all four
nodes), so the mount was the only reference. With that one step added, 0 of 36 laps hit the
abort. The module refcount behaved correctly. DISPROVED as an MXFS defect.
