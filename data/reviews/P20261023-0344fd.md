1. A lap that rejoins a victim on a real node through `pf_boot_rejoin`.
2. A probe on a kernel-log or `/sys/module/mxfs/parameters` line on the victim after the rejoin, showing that the load happened.
3. The batch, its control, the rate and every ledger row at the configuration the lap actually ran. The record names `8/disk/caw/mpath`, the configuration where the defect was observed.
4. If the rate is used at another configuration, a `--transfer` reason (docs/release-process.md:151-153) and exposure at that configuration too.
