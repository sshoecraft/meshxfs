## Check 4: the cause is proven by instrumented evidence, and the "fix" is shown to be correct. NOT MET

**(a) The check line passes by construction when the fix is on.** The record's check counts only
unheld-write lines that carry recov=1:

- tests/foreign_replay_ag_lap.sh:296 `[ -n "$R" ] && ck "$R reported its replay's AG header images apart (PROBE-A-REPLAY), none as an unheld write" \`
- tests/foreign_replay_ag_lap.sh:297 `    "$(cnt "$OUT/${R}_kmsg.txt" 'PROBE-A AG-META-WRITE-NOT-HELD.* recov=1')" 0`

With the knob at 1, the module sends every unheld write of a recov-tagged buffer to the
PROBE-A-REPLAY branch:

- pal/linux/xfs_buf.c:12018 `		if (!held && bp->b_mxfs_recov_image &&`
- pal/linux/xfs_buf.c:12019 `		    READ_ONCE(mxfs_probe_a_replay_apart)) {`
- pal/linux/xfs_buf.c:12031 `		} else if (!held) {`

So when the fix is on, a `... recov=1` unheld line cannot be printed at all. The 6/6 PASS shows
that the module was loaded with the knob at 1 and that the print moved. It does not show that
the detector now tells an authorised write from an unauthorised one. An unauthorised write of a
recov-tagged buffer would produce the same 6/6 PASS.

**(b) The exemption is wider than the record's own plan.** The record and the related major
record planned an exemption tied to the replay task:

- data/defects.json:6143 `... the detector then tells replay writes apart (b_mxfs_recov_image and the replay task) instead of firing on them.`
- data/defects.json:6143 `... the detector change (PROBE-A not firing on a recov=1 image written by the replay task) as its own fix.`

The fix as written (xfs_buf.c:12018-12019) tests only `bp->b_mxfs_recov_image` and the knob. It
does not check that the writer is the replay task (`m_mxfs_freplay_task`, which the read path does
test at xfs_buf.c:2589 `if (READ_ONCE(target->bt_mount->m_mxfs_freplay_task) ==`). It also does
not check that the replay is still running. The tag is set when the replay task reads the buffer
(xfs_buf.c:2594 `bp->b_mxfs_recov_image = true;`) and is cleared only by another task's fresh
read or by eviction. A tagged image that xfsaild writes back after the recovery-complete ladder has
released the victim's grant is the stale-image write PROBE-A exists to catch:

- xfs_buf.c:11980-11982 `... a buffer enters this node's AIL AFTER bast_work_fn released the grant ... and xfsaild later flushes the STALE in-core image to disk.`

That write would now print PROBE-A-REPLAY, with no stack. No evidence cited shows that this
cannot happen.

**(c) The lap does not check when the replay-labelled lines occurred.** The lap uses the window
[kill, ladder entry] only to collect AG numbers (tests/foreign_replay_ag_lap.sh:254
`        [ "$t" -ge "$T_KILL" ] && [ "$t" -le "$T_ENTRY" ] && grep -aoE 'agno=[0-9]+' <<<"$line" | cut -d= -f2`).
The `replay 6` count it prints (:256) covers the whole log since the mark, and no check
requires every PROBE-A-REPLAY line to fall inside the replay. Nothing cited proves that the 6 or
9 PROBE-A-REPLAY lines per fix-on lap were all written by the replay task, before its ladder
entry. The proposal cites no kernel-log lines, so no PROBE-A-REPLAY line with its comm= and
timestamp is in evidence.

## Verdict: REJECT

The control reproduced (10/10) and the fix-on batch is correctly sized (N=6) and passed 6/6.
But the check line it passes on cannot fail when the knob is on (xfs_buf.c:12018-12019 against
foreign_replay_ag_lap.sh:297). The claim that a replay image's write is authorised is the
unproven subject of the open major record D-FOREIGN-REPLAY-WRITES-AG-HEADERS-WITHOUT-THE-AG-LOCK
(data/defects.json:6141-6143). The fix also exempts any recov-tagged buffer, whichever task
writes it and whenever, which is wider than the replay-task exemption the records planned.
Removing the record as fixed would turn the always-on detector of a stale AG header write into
an unstacked info line exactly where an open major record says the write may be unauthorised.

What is needed before a removal can be approved:

1. A detector change that matches the plan: exempt only a write issued by the replay task
   (`m_mxfs_freplay_task == current`) while the replay is in progress, and keep reporting a
   recov-tagged write from any other task, or after the ladder, as AG-META-WRITE-NOT-HELD with its stack.
2. A lap check that fails when a PROBE-A-REPLAY line falls outside [kill, ladder entry] or comes
   from a task other than the replay task, and the kernel-log lines (comm=, timestamp) cited for
   each counted lap.
3. A control and verification on the same module build.
4. Either D-FOREIGN-REPLAY-WRITES-AG-HEADERS-WITHOUT-THE-AG-LOCK disposed of first, or the
   proposal shown not to depend on its question. As argued now ("the detector now tells a
   replay's authorised write from an unheld one"), it does depend on it.
