## Corrections and limits (do not change the verdict)

1. The record's evidence says the 8-node victim was "killed and rejoined, loading mxfs fresh". path_fenced_return
   rejoins with `pf_remount` (tests/mpath/path_fenced_return.sh:135 `pf_remount "$V" rejoin`; tests/mpath/lib.sh:497
   `timeout 40 umount $MNT; ... timeout 45 mount -t mxfs ...`), and the victim's kernel log runs on through one boot
   (kern_test14.txt:1 `[  634.954555]` to :13 `[  689.606314]`). The module was not reloaded. This does not change
   the cause: the global belongs to the module and survives a remount either way.
2. `ag_yield_drain_n=0` in all 32 laps of the four batches that reached the read, so no inline drain ran and the per-AG
   bound (:571) and the BAST prepass reading it (xfs/xfs_mxfs_ag_unlock.c:2331-2335) were never executed. The proposal
   says so and claims only what was measured, that the global is no longer left changed. This approval covers that
   claim and no claim about the per-AG path. Reading the code, the per-AG value cannot persist: the thread that sets
   it at :571 clears it at :579 `WRITE_ONCE(scan->pag_dlm_yield_stall_iters, 0);` after its own drain.
3. Fix-on lap 6 at 2/disk/caw/direct (09:12:07Z) ran slowly. It is accounted for under
   D-FOURKN-MERGE-WORKLOAD-OVERRAN-16S-WHEN-EVERY-CAW-TOOK-TWICE-AS-LONG, which names it, and it read 600 on both nodes
   with 275/263 overlaps.

  and another record accounts for it.
- The fix-on batches were sized from those controls (7 and 6 laps, recomputed from delta = alpha = 0.005). Both passed
  every lap with exposure, and both were reviewed and applied.

The removal disposes of this record as FIXED AND VERIFIED for the defect it states: overlapping yield scans no longer
leave the module-global stall bound changed. It does not cover the per-AG drain bound, which no lap exercised.
