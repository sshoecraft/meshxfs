## Verdict

**REJECT.**

The batch itself is sound. It was declared before its first lap; N = 18 matches delta = alpha = 0.005
applied to the 7/10 control (recomputed); all 18 laps are exposed PASSes on candidate 0.90.134
(build 468F7F30C2E190B0244AF70) with the pass seen in flight at NFILES=32000; none failed, none were
dropped, and the worst operation is under 30 s.

Approval still cannot be given, because applying it retires as "verified" runs that this evidence
does not account for:
1. 18 lap:jdt_gate_fault failures of 2026-10-23 (batches b20261022-172c3f and b20261022-4d455b).
   Each was killed at the 228 s bound (rc=124, abort=0), and its log ends at "A's lone remount", so
   no run shows the cause. The proposal does not name or cite them. A prior review already refused
   to retire them without per-run cause evidence.
2. 19 lap:jdt_gate_stall runs. That row runs its own NFILES=4000 manifest
   (tests/jdt_gate_stall_lap.py:59), which this fix does not change, and this batch never ran it. The
   argument also undercounts them (16 against the 19 that would be retired).

What would make this approvable: the 2026-10-23 cells taken out of this record's 2/net/mesh/direct
cells before the verify is applied, or each shown caused by this defect from its own run's evidence
(for example test1's kernel log from that lap's marker, with the P-TAUTH-PAGE-MINE count complete
before the first sample). The jdt_gate_stall cells must likewise be dropped from the record, or
backed by passing exposed laps of lap:jdt_gate_stall on this candidate with the pass pace in effect.
Then resubmit with an argument that lists every cell the verify will retire. The verify batch's
laps can be reused as they stand.
