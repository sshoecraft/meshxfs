Assorted group (loose fit): both notes concern the physical Proxmox pair, pve1 192.168.1.80 and pve2 192.168.1.81, and what must not be done to it.

**Disks: never the Kingstons.** After about a week of pair legs on the spare Kingston SSDNow V+ (SVP100S, 2011 SATA, QD1, ~1 s flush stalls), the user directed (2026-10-10) that no test, leg, A/B or measurement runs on them again; they are being removed. Fewer than 1% of users run SSDs that old. The reference disks are the Samsung 850 EVOs (pve1 250 GB, pve2 500 GB); wiping them for legs is authorized. Select by model ("850 EVO"), never by /dev name, since names shift when drives are removed. Failure mode: the D-REMOTE-NOQUEUE family reproduced only on the slow Kingstons, so every fix demanded a Kingston leg and a week was tuned to worst-case hardware. Defects seen only on such hardware stay in the queue but rank below anything seen on ordinary disks. Also: "working perfectly" meant the DRBD pair worked at 0.90.116 (released, failover suite green, no integrity/stability record), not every open slowdown record closed; do not inflate a criterion into an endless queue drain. See `feedback-never-test-on-the-2011-kingston-ssds-the-samsungs-are-the-pve-pair-reference-disks`.

**iSCSI helpers: never the rig ones on the pair.** The pair runs other work concurrently (DRBD legs on sdb, guests). `tools/lun_pool.sh alloc` logs the node out of every target, deletes all node records, fuser -km and umounts every mxfs, rmmods mxfs and rewrites multipath config; it also hands pool LUNs to rig runs that format them, and any image dropped in `/home/steve/disks/pool/` becomes allocatable. `scripts/pve_iscsi_login.sh` does `iscsiadm -m node -u` plus `-o delete` for ALL records. Both are for disposable rig VMs only. On the pair use `scripts/pve_caw_lun.sh` (setup/login/status/logout/teardown): image `/home/steve/disks/pve/pvecaw01.img` (outside the pool), target `iqn.2026-05.local.mxfs:pve-caw-01`, portal 192.168.1.166 only, LUN 0 in ini_group `pve`, targeted login with node.startup=automatic. SCST objects are runtime-only: rerun `setup` after a clyde restart. Verified 2026-10-10: both hosts /dev/sdd, same WWID, CAW cross-host, miscompare refused, PR key visible across hosts. See `trap-lun-pool-alloc-and-pve-iscsi-login-log-out-every-session-so-never-use-them-on-the-proxmox-pair`.
